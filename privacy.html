Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Knox Media Pvt. Ltd. ("Knox Media", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit knoxmedia.in or submit an inquiry through this website.

Information We Collect

When you submit the "Request Strategy Proposal" form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly:

  • Full name
  • Work email address
  • Phone number
  • Website URL
  • Your selected growth goal / service interest

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party ad pixels on this site beyond standard font loading (Google Fonts) and the analytics you may see referenced on the homepage for our client work, which relate to client accounts, not this website's visitors.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential engagement.
  • Follow up via email, phone, or WhatsApp using the contact details you provided.
  • Maintain an internal record of leads for our own business operations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Form submissions are stored securely on infrastructure we control. We retain inquiry data for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain business records, after which it may be deleted.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage only to remember a light/dark theme preference on your device. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to us.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've submitted to us by emailing contact@knoxmedia.in. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
